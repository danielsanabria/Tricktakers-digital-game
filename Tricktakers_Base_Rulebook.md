# GAME OVERVIEW

## トリックテイカーズ
# TRICKTAKERS

Every decade a tournament is held to decide who will be the next greatest Tricktaker. To prepare for this tournament, contenders spend years developing their own unique set of skills.

As the final battle approaches, these eight are all that remain...

&lt;img&gt;Eight Tricktakers characters&lt;/img&gt;

Players will be signing “contracts” with one of the eight characters each round in order to gain the upper hand in winning the most tricks... or possibly wanting no tricks at all. Using their character’s abilities, players will be able to draw new cards during the round, change the hierarchy of suit strength, use items to manipulate their cards, gain access to the highest cards in each suit, and more!

A game of TRICKTAKERS typically consists of 3 rounds. In each round, players will select a character that they will use for the round after they are dealt 5 cards. Players will then play 5 tricks, in clockwise order. After 5 tricks, players will gain crowns and/or score points according to their characters. After 3 rounds, the player with the highest score wins. However, this is not always the case since there are three other ways to win the game!

---

## WINNING CONDITIONS

### 1 Fulfill A Character's Win Condition
Each character has their own winning condition. After fulfilling the condition, that player wins the game.

*   5 tricks Win the game
*   9 Straight Flush Win the game
*   4 bid Win the game
*   5 tricks Win the game
*   Any Black card Win the game

### 2 Have 2 Gold Crowns
The player who wins the most tricks in a round gains a gold crown. After gaining 2 gold crowns, that player wins the game.

&lt;img&gt;Two gold crowns&lt;/img&gt;

### 3 Have 3 Black Crowns
A player who wins no tricks in a round gains a black crown. After gaining 3 black crowns, that player wins the game.

&lt;img&gt;Three black crowns&lt;/img&gt;

### 4 Have The Most Points
If no player has fulfilled 1, 2, or 3 after 3 rounds, the player with the most points wins the game.

&lt;img&gt;Four point icons (50, 50, 10, 10)&lt;/img&gt;

*   If multiple winning conditions are met in the same round, the earlier condition wins (1 > 2 > 3 > 4).
*   If multiple players accomplish the same winning condition at the same time, the player who has the higher priority character wins.

Example: In Round 3, the King wins 5 tricks and the Berserker wins 0 tricks. This triggers Winning Condition 1 for both characters. However, the King wins the game because they have the higher priority.

# HOW TO PLAY: UNDERSTANDING A TRICK AND DECK MAKEUP

A "trick" refers to all players, in clockwise order, taking turns playing one card from their hand to the middle of the table. When starting a new trick, the first person to play a card is known as the "lead player." The lead player may play any one card from their hand. All other players "must follow" suit, i.e., play one card of the same color as the lead player, if possible. If a player cannot follow suit, they may play any card, known as "playing off suit." After all cards are played, the person who played the card with the highest strength wins the trick. If two or more cards with the same strength are played in a trick, the first one played wins.
The winner of the trick collects all of the played cards, puts them in a face down pile in front of themselves, and then becomes the lead player for the next trick.

&lt;img&gt;Three stacks of cards&lt;/img&gt;

When winning multiple tricks, be sure to keep each trick pile separate. This will make it easier to count the number of tricks you have won!

There are 40 cards in the deck, which consists of Number cards, Rare cards, and White Flag cards.

*   **36 Number Cards**
    *   4 colored suits, numbered 1 to 9
    *   &lt;img&gt;Red Number Card (9)&lt;/img&gt; &lt;img&gt;Blue Number Card (8)&lt;/img&gt; &lt;img&gt;Green Number Card (7)&lt;/img&gt; &lt;img&gt;Black Number Card (6)&lt;/img&gt;
    *   &lt;img&gt;Red Number Card (5)&lt;/img&gt; &lt;img&gt;Blue Number Card (4)&lt;/img&gt; &lt;img&gt;Green Number Card (3)&lt;/img&gt; &lt;img&gt;Black Number Card (2)&lt;/img&gt;

*   **Card Strength**
    *   When determining the winner of the trick, you need to compare the strength of each card played. When comparing Number Cards, normally, the larger the number, the stronger the card (e.g. Blue 9 > Blue 5 > Blue 1).
    *   However, when comparing the Red, Blue, and Green suits, the lead suit is considered to be stronger than the other two. Additionally, the Black suit is the trump suit, meaning it is stronger than the Red, Blue, and Green suits.
    *   Note: The Black (trump) suit can be lead at any time and does not need to be "broken," meaning played off suit, first.

## Must Follow

When a player has a card in hand that matches the color of the lead suit, they must play it. If they have 2 or more cards of the same color, they can choose which card to play. If they don't have any cards in hand that match the lead suit, they can play any card from hand.

&lt;img&gt;Hand with Blue 5 as Lead&lt;/img&gt;
The lead player plays a Red 5. If this was your hand, you would be able to play any card since you do not have a Red card in hand.

# HOW TO PLAY: ROUND 1 (3-5 PLAYERS)

&lt;img&gt;Game Preparation&lt;/img&gt;

## 1 Deal Playing Cards

Deal 5 cards to each player from the draw deck. Players should look at the cards that are dealt to them, but make sure to keep them hidden from the other players.

## 2 Select a Character

Starting from the lead player and going clockwise, each player selects a face up character card from the middle of the table and places it face up in front of themselves. Any unselected characters should be set aside for now.

## 3 Set Up Characters

Setting up each character is completed in character priority order, highest first (1A, 2A, 3A...). However, to help speed up the process, players may agree to do this step simultaneously. Each character is explained in more detail on pages 12-27.

## 4 Play Tricks and Check For A Character's Win Condition

Play 5 tricks as described on pages 6-7. Then, check to see if any player has fulfilled their character's win condition. If so, that player wins immediately! If no character's win condition has been met, proceed to steps 5 - 8.
Note: Keep in mind the Resistance (3A) character can fulfill their win condition during the round!
Reminder: If multiple players have accomplished their character's win condition, the winner is determined by the higher character priority.

## 5 Gain Gold Crown &lt;img&gt;Gold Crown Icon&lt;/img&gt;

The player who won the most tricks in the round will gain a gold crown. If there is a tie for the most tricks, no one gets the gold crown for that round and it is returned to the box instead.

## 6 Gain Black Crown &lt;img&gt;Black Crown Icon&lt;/img&gt;

Any player that won zero tricks in the round will be eligible to gain a black crown. Only 2 players can gain a black crown. If there are 3 or more eligible players to gain a black crown, players will gain them in higher character priority order. Any leftover black crowns should be returned to the box (this will happen if 0 players or only 1 player gains a black crown).

## 7 Gain Points and Gain Lead Player Token &lt;img&gt;50 Points Icon&lt;/img&gt; &lt;img&gt;10 Points Icon&lt;/img&gt; &lt;img&gt;Lead Player Token Icon&lt;/img&gt;

All players will gain (or lose) points according to the conditions written on their character card. Give the lead player token to the player with the least amount of points and no gold crown. If there is a tie for points, the player with the higher character priority takes the token. This player will get to choose who leads the first trick in the next round.
Note: Players cannot have less than 0 points. If you were to lose points and go below 0, stop at 0 points instead.

## 8 Check For A Crown Victory

After crowns and points are handed out, check to see if any player has 2 gold crowns or 3 black crowns. If so, that player wins immediately! If no player has 2 gold crowns or 3 black crowns, proceed to the next round.
Note: This can happen in Round 1 when using some of the promo or expansion characters!

# HOW TO PLAY: ROUND 2 [3-5 PLAYERS]

1.  **Deal Playing Cards**
    Gather all 40 playing cards, shuffle them, and place them in the middle of the table creating a new draw deck. Deal 5 cards to each player.

2.  **Select a Character**
    All players will return the character they used in the previous round to the middle of the table, along with the unselected characters that were set aside earlier. Next, players will select a new character for this round in the order specified below. Players may select the same character they selected in the previous round, except for the King.
    Players will select characters in the following order:

    *   The player who selected the King in the previous round. They cannot select the King again this round.
    *   The players who do not have a gold crown, starting with the player who has the lowest number of points. If there is a tie, the player who selected a character from the previous round with a higher priority will go first.
        Example: Players A, B, and C do not have gold crowns. Player B has 30 points, while Players A and C are tied with 80 points each. Player B will select a character first. Then, Players A and C need to compare their character priority from the previous round. Player A was the Gambler (2A) and Player C was the Hermit (4A). Therefore, Player A will select a character before Player C.
    *   The players who have a gold crown, starting with the player who has the lowest number of points. If there is a tie, the player who selected a character from the previous round with a higher priority will go first.

    Note: Black crowns are not taken into consideration during the selection process stated above. Only gold crowns are taken into consideration.

3.  **Set Up Characters**
    Set up each character as described in Round 1.

4.  **Play Tricks and Check For A Character’s Win Condition**
    Play tricks and check for a character’s win condition as described in Round 1, with one difference: Whoever has the lead player token will choose the lead player for the first trick, which can include themselves.

5.  **Gain Gold Crown, Gain Black Crown, Gain Points, and Gain Lead Player Token**
    Players will gain crowns, points, and the lead player token as described in Round 1.
    Reminder: The player who won the most tricks in the round (no ties) will gain the gold crown. Only 2 players who won 0 tricks in the round will gain a black crown (ties are broken by higher character priority). All players will gain or lose points based on their character.

6.  **Check For A Crown Victory**
    After players gain crowns and points, check for a crown victory as described in Round 1.


# HOW TO PLAY: ROUND 3 [3-5 PLAYERS]

1.  **Deal Playing Cards**
    Deal out the playing cards as described in Round 2.

2.  **Select a Character**
    Select a character as described in Round 2.

3.  **Set Up Characters and Spend Black Crowns** &lt;img&gt;Discard icon&lt;/img&gt; = Discard
    Set up each character as described in Round 1, with one difference: While you are setting up your character, if you have a black crown, you may return it to the game box to discard any number of cards from your hand, face down, and draw that many back from the draw deck. If you have a second black crown, you may do it again.

4.  **Play Tricks and Check For A Character’s Win Condition**
    Play tricks and check for a character’s win condition as described in Round 2.

5.  **Gain Gold Crown, Gain Black Crown, and Gain Points** &lt;img&gt;Gold Crown icon&lt;/img&gt; &lt;img&gt;Black Crown icon&lt;/img&gt; &lt;img&gt;50 Points icon&lt;/img&gt; &lt;img&gt;10 Points icon&lt;/img&gt;
    Players will gain crowns and points as described in Round 1, with one difference: Each basic character has a “Round 3” ability that may change the way they gain points.
    Reminder: The player who won the most tricks in the round (no ties) will gain the gold crown. Only 2 players who won 0 tricks in the round will gain a black crown (ties are broken by higher character priority). All players will gain or lose points based on their character.

6.  **Check For A Crown Victory**
    After players gain crowns and points, check for a crown victory as described in Round 1. If no player has won, then proceed to step 7.

7.  **Calculate Points and Determine Final Winner**
    All players will calculate the number of points they have and the player with the most points wins! If there is a tie, the player with the higher priority character wins.

The next few pages will explain the unique abilities of each character. Many of these abilities will break the normal rules of the game, so it is very important to remember:
If a character text or ability directly contradicts any text from the rulebook so far, the text or ability of the character takes precedence!

**Revolt**
During 'Step 3: Set Up Characters,' place the Revolt Token next to your character card. Once per round, on your turn during 'Step 4: Play Tricks,' you may place the Revolt Token on the card you just played. By doing so, the card strength hierarchy is reversed for this trick (the lowest strength card wins instead of the highest strength). This trick is known as the 'Revolt Trick.' After the Revolt Trick is over, set the Revolt Token aside. It cannot be used again this round. During the Revolt Trick keep these four things in mind:

*   The lead suit advantage between the Red, Blue, and Green suits is nullified. Normally, if Blue was the lead suit, Blue is stronger than Red and Green, regardless of the number. However, during the Revolt Trick, the suits become equal strength and it comes down to the lowest number played. **You still must follow suit when playing!**
    *   Note: Also keep in mind the Black suit is a higher strength and will lose to the Red, Blue, and Green suits during a Revolt trick.
*   The effect of the Hermit's White Flag being stronger than a Rare card (explained on page 16) is nullified.
*   The effect of the 1s being stronger than the Berserker's 10s or the Berserker card (explained on page 17) is nullified. Simply put, during the Revolt Trick, there are no card strengthening abilities and the weakest (or lowest strength) card wins.
*   If there is a tie for the lowest strength card in the Revolt Trick, the first one played wins as normal.
*   If you win the Revolt Trick, depending on the card you win with, you will score a varying amount of points.

See page 19 for more Revolt Trick examples.

## Giving Tasks
During 'Step 3: Set Up Characters,' after all players have finished setting up their characters, you must give each other player 1 task card. There are two types of task cards (Normal and Hard).

Normal tasks - Do not take a Red, Do not take a Blue, Do not take a Green
Hard tasks - Take a Red, Take a Blue, Take a Green

*   If a player completes their task by the end of the round, they will gain points as shown on their task card. Additionally, when playing with 3-5 players, the Ruler will also gain points from every completed task.
*   If a player does not complete their task by the end of the round, they will lose points as shown on their task card. However, the Ruler will not lose any points from uncompleted tasks.
*   After a player completes a hard task during the round, flip the task card face down to show it has been completed. Normal tasks cannot be completed until after all 5 tricks are resolved.

As the Ruler, you give:
*   The King the 'Take a Blue' task,
*   The Hermit the 'Do not take a Red' task

*   All tasks are completed: +10 points

During 'Step 3: Set Up Characters,' place the 4 Lead Tokens next to your character card. # CHARACTER STRATEGY

## The more tricks you win, the higher your score will be! (EASY)
&lt;img&gt;A character with purple hair and a crown.&lt;/img&gt;
The extra Rare card makes it easier for you to win tricks. You want to win as many tricks as possible to get a lot of points and the gold crown. You should consider taking the King when you have a strong hand. Also, you earn first priority to select a character for the next round. If you gained a gold crown in the previous round as the King, it may be a good idea to choose a character that has an easier time winning tricks, such as the Berserker or Adventurer, aiming for the second gold crown.

## You want to focus on gaining the gold crown rather than points! (MODERATE)
&lt;img&gt;A character with horns and a chainmail outfit.&lt;/img&gt;
The Berserker's exclusive deck is very strong. But watch out—other players know what cards are in the Berserker deck and can have an idea of what you might play. Since you are weak to the 1s, it helps to check how many '1' cards you have before discarding your hand. You don't get as many points as the King from winning tricks, so it is better for you to be aiming for the gold crown rather than points. Just be careful not to win all 5 tricks or else you will lose points.

## You want to aim for getting the highest score! (MODERATE)
&lt;img&gt;A fox-like character with a mustache and a hat.&lt;/img&gt;
If you don't like your initial hand, the Gambler may be a good choice. This is because you can exchange your cards before playing tricks in each round. The more cards you exchange, the more information you can get about what is not in the other player's hands. If you bid for 1 or 2 trick wins, bet high and win big! If you bid for 2 or 3 trick wins, you may not want to bet.

## Use items to your advantage, some unused items are negative points! (DIFFICULT)
&lt;img&gt;A raccoon-like character with a hat and a backpack.&lt;/img&gt;
Each time you win a trick you will get a new equipment card. So, you should try to win as many tricks as possible from the beginning. However, you may lose points by not using some of your items. What you draw from the item deck will affect the Adventurer's playstyle. You may or may not want to win the 5th trick, because the item you draw could have negative points. If you don't like your initial hand, consider choosing the "Map of Destiny" as your initial blue equipment card and using it in the first trick.

## Try to lose 4 tricks and then win big during the Revolt! (MODERATE)
&lt;img&gt;A character with rabbit ears and a bow.&lt;/img&gt;
The Revolt is a powerful ability that changes the card strength hierarchy. The other players need to be careful about leading with the Black suit, as you win the game when you win the Revolt Trick with a Black card. The Revolt is declared as you play your card, so it is usually a good idea to declare it when you are the last to play a card in the trick. Be sure to only win the Revolt Trick to be eligible for a black crown.

## Make sure to win at least 1 trick, and make at least 2 combinations! (MODERATE)
&lt;img&gt;A character with blonde hair and a feathered headpiece.&lt;/img&gt;
You cannot gain gold or black crowns, so getting as high of a score as possible is the main strategy. Since you can stop other players from gaining the gold crown, trying to win as many tricks as possible is a good idea. Getting around 50 points is good, and 80+ is best, so you should aim for combinations around that score. You will always get the card you reserved, so keep that in mind when deciding what combination you are trying to make. Aiming for a "straight flush" can be a good start.

## Normally you want 0 tricks to gain a lot points and a black crown! (EASY)
&lt;img&gt;A character with blue hair and a headband.&lt;/img&gt;
Before your play, you can draw a card, and discard a card. There is a high chance you will be able to avoid the must follow rules. Try your best to lose all the tricks and get 50 points and a black crown. However, the other players will likely anticipate this, so you can surprise them by winning 3+ tricks if your hand is strong. If you plan on winning tricks, be sure to win with a White Flag when there is a Rare card in play.

## You will want to be familiar with the abilities of every character! (DIFFICULT)
&lt;img&gt;A character with long blonde hair and a crown.&lt;/img&gt;
How do you distribute the task cards? Typically, you should give the Hard tasks to characters such as the King and Berserker, while giving Normal tasks to the Resistance and Hermit. You can initially ignore the must follow rule and should use that to your advantage in helping other characters complete their tasks or to help you win tricks. Winning 2 tricks without Red, Blue, or Green cards is very difficult, so try to hold back any Black or Rare cards until the moment is right!

## RULER VARIANT (3-5 PLAYER ONLY) &lt;img&gt;Ruler Variant Icon&lt;/img&gt;

All gameplay rules are the same, except for the following changes:

*   In 3-player games, the Ruler can give out 1 or 2 task cards to the other players. This means you can give both players 1 task card, one player 1 task card and the other player 2 task cards, or both players 2 task cards.
    The Ruler can gain additional points:
    *   All tasks are completed: +30 points
    *   Take a Black card in any trick: +10 points
    *   Only win 1 trick in the round¹: +20 points and gain the gold crown
    *   Only win 1 trick in the round that does not have Red, Blue, and Green cards²: Win the game!
    ¹If you only win 1 trick in the round, during 'Step 5: Gain Gold Crown' you gain that round's gold crown. You gain the gold crown regardless of who would have been eligible for it. If you are playing with the 3 player Resistance variant, and both characters are eligible for the gold crown, the Resistance would gain the gold crown since they have higher character priority.
    ²If you only win 1 trick in the round, but there are Red, Blue, and/or Green cards in the trick, you do not win the game, but you can still gain the gold crown. Additionally, if you win 2 or more tricks in the round, even if there are no Red, Blue, and/or Green cards in all tricks, you do not win the game.

## RULER VARIANT TASK CARDS (3-5 PLAYER ONLY) &lt;img&gt;Ruler character icon&lt;/img&gt;

When using the new task cards, you may add them to the existing 6 task cards or replace them completely. There is a new type of task card: Difficult, which is worth 30 points if completed.

These tasks are mainly designed to include some variability in the task choices for the Ruler. However, they are also designed to help learn some of the other character's strengths and weaknesses a little quicker. While all variants are only recommended to be used after playing the original game multiple times, this variant can be helpful if you have one or more experienced players along with new players at the table. The Ruler tends to be the most difficult character to play since it requires a good understanding of all other characters. Therefore, these tasks should help all players, new or experienced, identify what each character is typically aiming for in the round.

# GAME SUMMARY (3-5 PLAYERS)

1.  **Deal Playing Cards**
    *   5 cards per player

2.  **Select a Character**
    *   Round 1: Clockwise from lead player
    *   Round 2/3: King > No Gold Crown > Gold Crown

3.  **Set Up Characters**
    *   See pages 12-27

4.  **Play Tricks/Check Character's Win Condition**
    *   Starting from the lead player, and going clockwise, each player plays one card, following the lead suit, if possible.
    *   The highest strength card in play wins the trick. If it is a Revolt Trick, the lowest strength card in play wins instead.
    *   Play 5 tricks.
    *   Check to see if any player completed their character's win condition.

5.  **Gain Crowns, Points, and Lead Player Token**
    *   The player who won the most tricks will gain the gold crown
    *   Up to 2 players who won 0 tricks will gain a black crown
    *   All players will gain or lose points based on their character
    *   The player with the least amount of points, and no gold crown, gains the Lead Player Token

# WINNING CONDITIONS (3-5 PLAYERS)

1.  **Character's Win Condition**

2.  **Have 2 Gold Crowns**

3.  **Have 3 Black Crowns**

4.  **Have The Most Points**
    *   If no player has fulfilled 1, 2, or 3 after 3 rounds, the player with the most points wins the game.

If multiple winning conditions are met at in the same round, the earlier condition wins (1 > 2 > 3 > 4).

If multiple players have accomplished the same winning condition at the same time, the player who has the higher priority character wins.
