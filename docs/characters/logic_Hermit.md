# Hermit (4A)

## Overview
*   **Rank:** 4A
*   **Difficulty:** Moderate
*   **Key Mechanic:** Hand Filtering & Counter-Play

## Abilities

### Dexterous Hand
Before playing a card in **every trick**:
1.  **Draw 1** card from the draw deck.
2.  **Discard 1** card from your hand.
    *   *Usage:* This allows you to fish for White Flags or specific suits.

### Sage's Wisdom (White Flag Priority)
If you play a **White Flag** and another player plays a **Rare Card**:
*   Your White Flag beats the Rare Card (reversing the normal rule where Rare > White Flag).
*   This applies unless other overrides (like Revolt) interfere.

### Bonus Points
*   **Counter-Snipe:** If you win a trick using a White Flag beating a Rare Card, you immediately gain **+30 pts**.
*   **Round 3 Boost:** In Round 3, this bonus increases to **+100 pts**.

## Win Conditions
1.  **5 Tricks:** If you win all 5 tricks, you instantly win the game.

## Scoring
Scoring depends on the number of tricks won:

| Tricks Won | Points |
| :--- | :--- |
| **0** | +50 |
| **1** | -10 |
| **2** | -30 |
| **3** | +70 |
| **4** | +100 |
| **5** | Win Game |
