# Ruler (5B)

## Overview
*   **Rank:** 5B
*   **Difficulty:** Difficult
*   **Key Mechanic:** Rule Manipulation & Task Assignment

## Abilities

### Giving Tasks (Setup)
During **Step 3: Setup**, distribute **Task Cards** to opponents:
*   **3-Player Game:** Give 1-2 Tasks per opponent.
*   **4-5 Player Game:** Give 1 Task per opponent.
*   **Types:**
    *   **Normal:** "Do not take Red/Blue/Green".
    *   **Hard:** "Take a Red/Blue/Green".

### Rule Avoidance
On your turn, you may:
1.  **Play Any Card:** Ignore the lead suit requirement initially.
2.  **Declare Suit:** Place a **Lead Token** on the played card to define its suit for the trick.
    *   *Note:* You can change the lead suit if you are the leader!

## Win Conditions
1.  **Colorless Victory:** If you win **2 or more tricks** and **none of the won tricks contain a colored card** (Red, Blue, Green), you **instantly win the game**.
    *   Captured cards must be Black, Rare, White Flag, or Berserker.

## Scoring
Scoring comes from tasks and trick management:

### Task Bonuses
*   **Completion:** +10 pts (Normal) / +20 pts (Hard) for *each* task completed by an opponent.
*   **All Complete Bonus:** +10 pts if **all** assigned tasks are completed.
*   **Wait:** Opponents usually lose points for failing tasks, but Ruler gains nothing from failures. Ruler wants them to succeed.

### Ruler's Bonuses
*   **Black Card:** +10 pts if you capture at least one Black Card.
*   **Single Win:** +20 pts if you win exactly **1 trick**.
