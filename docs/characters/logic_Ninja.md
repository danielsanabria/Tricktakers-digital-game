# Ninja (2D)

## Overview
*   **Rank:** 2D
*   **Difficulty:** High (Tricky)
*   **Key Mechanic:** Shadow Cloning (Extra Card Play)

## Abilities

### Shadow Cloning (Action Phase)
In your turn, you use a special flow:
1.  **Play 1 Card:** Play a card from your hand normally.
2.  **Play Face Down:** You may choose to play a second card **Face Down** (draw 1 card from deck).
    *   *Note:* This face-down card effectively "clones" your presence but its power is 0.
3.  **Resolve:**
    *   After everyone has played, decide to **reveal** or **discard** the face-down card.
    *   If valid (Must Follow), it participates. If not, discard.
    *   *Strategic:* Allows you to play 2 cards to dump hand or bluff.

### 5th Trick Special
For the **5th Trick**:
*   Look at 1 card from the deck and play it face down.

### Black Crown Hunter
Ninja can acquire a **Black Crown** with **2 Wins** (instead of the usual 0).

## Win Conditions
1.  **5 Tricks:** If you win all 5 tricks, you instantly win the game.
