# Adventurer (3B)

## Overview
*   **Rank:** 3B
*   **Difficulty:** Difficult
*   **Key Mechanic:** Dynamic Item Use & Level Up

## Abilities

### Initial Equipment (Setup)
During **Step 3: Setup**:
1.  Choose **1 Red Item** and **1 Blue Item** from the starting options.
2.  Shuffle the remaining starting items + regular items to form the **Item Deck**.

### Using Items
Before playing a card in a trick, you may use **1 Item** from your available equipment slots.
*   **Effect:** Resolves immediately (modifies card, draws cards, etc.).
*   **Restock:** Draw a new item from the Item Deck to replace the used one.

### Level Up (Trick Victory)
When you **win a trick**:
1.  The pile of played cards becomes a **New Equipment Slot**.
2.  **Draw** a new item immediately to fill this slot.

## Items List (Brief)
*   **Red (Power):** Berserker's Axe (Set to 10), Ruler's Wand (Match Lead Suit), Miracle Sword (+/- 5). generally -30 pts if unused.
*   **Blue (Utility):** Map of Destiny (Mulligan), Invisibility Potion (Face Down), Timid Boots (Play Last). generally +10 pts if unused.
*   **Regular:** Hermit's Book, White Orb, Proactive Wing, Dragon Doll, Fairy Mischief, Gold, Castle Visit, Crystal, Rock Crystal.

## Win Conditions
1.  **5 Tricks:** If you win all 5 tricks, you instantly win the game.

## Scoring
Scoring consists of Trick Points + Item Points.

### Trick Points
| Tricks Won | Points |
| :--- | :--- |
| **0** | +20 |
| **1** | +10 |
| **2** | +20 |
| **3** | +40 |
| **4** | +60 |
| **5** | Win Game |

### Item Points (End of Round)
Unused items on your equipment slots grant points:
*   **Passive/Utility Items:** Usually **+10 pts**.
*   **Aggressive/Power Items:** Usually **-30 pts**.
*   **Special:**
    *   Crystal: +20 (Initial) / +30 (Extra Slot).
    *   Rock Crystal: +30 (Initial) / +50 (Extra Slot).
    *   Fairy Mischief: -30 pts.
