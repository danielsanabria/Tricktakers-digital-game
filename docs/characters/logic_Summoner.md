# Summoner (2C)

## Overview
*   **Rank:** 2C
*   **Difficulty:** High
*   **Key Mechanic:** MP Management & Beast Summoning

## Abilities

### Mana Point (MP) System
*   **Start:** 5 MP.
*   **Gain:** +1 MP every time you **Win a Trick**.

### Summoning (Action Phase)
In your turn, follow this sequence:
1.  **Summon (Rear):** Pay MP to place beasts in Rearguard.
2.  **Command:** Use Rearguard abilities (e.g., Draw/Discard).
3.  **Play:** Play a card from hand.
4.  **Advance (Front):** Move a Beast to Front (on top of played card).

### Beasts
*   **EL (Phoenix):** Cost 4. Rear: +1 MP/turn. Front: **White Flag** (Beats Rare).
*   **MIRIA (Dragon):** Cost 3. Rear: **10 beats 1** (Global). Front: **Berserker** status.
*   **Colors (Maru/Guru/Nemu):** Cost 1. Front: **10** of matching suit.
*   **OKO (Black):** Cost 1. Front: **Black 10**.

### Movement Cost
*   Moving to Front is **Free** if the played card matches the Beast's Color/Suit.
*   If not matching, checking rules (usually extra MP cost, e.g. +2 for Black).

## Win Conditions
1.  **5 Tricks:** If you win all 5 tricks, you instantly win the game.
