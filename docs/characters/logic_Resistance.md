# Resistance (3A)

## Overview
*   **Rank:** 3A
*   **Difficulty:** Moderate
*   **Key Mechanic:** Revolution (Reversing Strength)

## Abilities

### Revolt (Kakumei)
During **Step 3: Setup**, take the **Revolt Token**.
Once per round, when playing a card, you may place the Revolt Token on it to declare a **Revolt Trick**.
*   **Effect:** For this trick only, **Lowest Strength Wins**.
    *   Lead suit advantage is nullified (suits are equal).
    *   Hermit's White Flag > Rare rule is nullified.
    *   1 > 10 rule is nullified.
    *   **Black Suit:** Becomes weaker than Color suits (Red/Blue/Green) in a Revolt.
    *   **Tiebreaker:** First played still wins ties? (Needs verification, standard rules usually imply first played wins ties).

### Battle Ready (Round 3)
In Round 3, you gain **30 points** for every trick you win (including the Revolt Trick).

## Win Conditions
1.  **Revolt Win with Black:** If you win the **Revolt Trick** using a **Black Card**, you win the game immediately.

## Scoring
Points are awarded for winning the **Revolt Trick** based on the card used:

| Card Used | Points |
| :--- | :--- |
| **White Flag** | +30 pts |
| **1, 2, 3** (RGB) | +50 pts |
| **4, 5, 6** (RGB) | +80 pts |
| **7, 8, 9** (RGB) | +100 pts |
| **Black Card** | **Win Game** |

*   **Normal Trick Wins:** 0 pts (unless Round 3 ability applies).
