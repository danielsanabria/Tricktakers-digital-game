# Phantom Thief (5C)

## Overview
*   **Rank:** 5C (Lowest Priority)
*   **Difficulty:** High
*   **Key Mechanic:** Partnership & Theft

## Abilities

### Notices & Invitation (Setup)
*   **Notices:** Give to opponents you want to steal from.
*   **Invitation:** Give to **1 Partner**.
*   **Phantom Chip:** Set to **0** or **±1**. (Target difference in wins).

### Exchange (Action)
On your turn, you may exchange 1 card with your **Partner**.
*   You offer a card face down.
*   Partner accepts (gives you one) or declines.
*   If declined, discard yours and draw 1.

### Betrayal / Chip Flip (Trick Win)
After winning a trick, you may:
*   **Reselect:** Change Chip between 0 and ±1.
*   **Flip (Wolf):** Betray Partner. Now you only steal from the Partner.

## Win Conditions
1.  **5 Tricks:** If you win all 5 tricks, you instantly win the game.

## Scoring
*   **Steal:** If (YourWins - TargetWins) matches Chip (0 or ±1), you steal **Crown** or **30 pts** from them.
*   **Trick Points:**
    *   **1 Win:** Eligible for Black Crown (instead of 0 wins). -20 pts.
    *   **2 Wins:** **Partner** gains +50 pts (You get 0).
    *   **Other:** Standard scoring? (Rulebook implies specific thief scoring is sparse, focuses on stealing).
