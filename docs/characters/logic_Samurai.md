# Samurai (3D)

## Overview
*   **Rank:** 3D
*   **Difficulty:** Moderate
*   **Key Mechanic:** Spirit of Red & Hand Purity

## Abilities

### Hand Purity (Passive)
The Samurai **cannot hold Black cards**.
*   **Setup:** Discard all Black cards from your initial hand and redraw equal number.
*   **During Game:** If you receive a Black card (e.g., from Time Traveler), you must immediately discard it and draw a replacement.

### Spirit of Red (Passive)
When you play a **Red card**, its strength is treated as equal to a **Black card** (Trump suit).
*   *Note:* It is still Red suit for "Must Follow" rules, but its power in comparison is Black tier.
*   **Exceptions (Nullified):** This ability is **disabled** if:
    *   A **White Flag** is in play.
    *   A **Revolution** (Kakumei) is active.

### Take Red Card (Trick Win)
When you **win a trick**:
1.  You may take **1 Red Card** played by another player in that trick into your hand.
2.  If you do, you must **discard 1 card** from your hand.

## Win Conditions
1.  **4 Tricks:** If you win exactly **4 tricks** in a round, you **instantly win the game**.

## Scoring
Scoring rewards consistency but punishes greed (5 wins).

| Tricks Won | Points |
| :--- | :--- |
| **0** | 0 |
| **1** | +30 |
| **2** | +80 |
