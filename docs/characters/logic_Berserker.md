# Berserker (5A)

## Overview
*   **Rank:** 5A
*   **Difficulty:** Moderate
*   **Key Mechanic:** Overwhelming Power (with a weakness)

## Abilities

### Berserker Deck (Setup)
During **Step 3: Setup**:
1.  **Discard your entire hand** (5 cards).
2.  **Draw** the **Berserker Card** + **4 Random Cards** from the Berserker Exclusive Deck.

### Raw Power
*   **The Berserker Card** beats **EVERYTHING** (even Rares), **EXCEPT**:
    *   It loses to any **1** (Red 1, Blue 1, Green 1, Black 1).
*   **Revolt Interaction:** During a Revolt (Resistance), the Berserker is the **weakest** card.

### Fierce Uplifting (Round 3)
In Round 3, if you have a **Black Crown**, you may spend it during Setup to:
*   Discard up to 2 cards from your hand (cannot discard Berserker Card).
*   Draw replacements from the remaining cards in the Berserker Deck.

## Win Conditions
1.  **0 Tricks (Round 3):** If you win **0 tricks** in Round 3, you **instantly win the game**.

## Scoring
Scoring forces you to go big or go home (or go for 0).
