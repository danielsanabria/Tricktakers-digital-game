# Time Traveler (4C)

## Overview
*   **Rank:** 4C
*   **Difficulty:** Expert
*   **Key Mechanic:** Rewinding & Changing History

## Abilities

### Predictions (Setup)
*   Predict who will win the **Gold Crown** (1 player).
*   Predict who will win the **Black Crowns** (2 players).
*   *Resource:* Start with **2 Time Tokens**.

### Rewind (Action)
On your turn, spend **1 Token** to:
1.  **Return** all played cards in the trick to owners' hands.
2.  **Draw 2** cards from deck, **Discard 2** cards.
3.  Choose the **Lead Player** for the restarted trick.
*   *Note:* Does not undo point loss (Traps) or MP spent (Summoner).

### Change the Past (Trick Win)
After winning a trick (except the 5th), spend **1 Token** to:
1.  **Take ALL** cards played in the trick into your hand.
2.  **Give 1 Card** to each opponent from your hand (can be any card).
3.  Choose the **Lead Player** for the next trick.

## Win Conditions
1.  **Prophecy Fulfilled (Round 3):** If your Setup predictions (Gold + 2 Black Crowns) are perfectly correct in Round 3, you **instantly win the game**.

## Scoring
*   **Predictions:** +50 pts for each correct prediction (Gold/Black Crown winners) at end of round.
