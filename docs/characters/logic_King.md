# King (1A)

## Overview
*   **Rank:** 1A (Highest Priority)
*   **Difficulty:** Easy
*   **Key Mechanic:** Hand Manipulation & Priority

## Abilities

### King's Rare Card
During **Step 3: Setup**, you must add the **King's Rare Card** to your hand and discard any 1 card from your hand.
*   The King's Rare Card is a strong card but can be beaten by certain abilities (e.g., Hermit's White Flag).

### King's Privilege
Regardless of who has the Gold Crown or the most points, the player who played the King in the current round **selects their character first** in the next round.
*   **Restriction:** You cannot select the King character two rounds in a row.

### The Final Battle (Round 3)
In Round 3, the points you gain from the character card (based on tricks won) are **doubled**.

## Win Conditions
1.  **5 Tricks:** If you win all 5 tricks in a round, you instantly win the game.

## Scoring
*   **0 Wins:** 0 pts
*   **1 Win:** 20 pts
*   **2 Wins:** 50 pts
*   **3 Wins:** 80 pts
*   **4 Wins:** 120 pts
*   **5 Wins:** Win Game

*(In Round 3, double these point values.)*
